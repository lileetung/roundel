# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-20

First public release.

- A macOS menu bar calendar that marks a day with a ring, and unmarks it on a
  second click.
- A dashboard drawing the whole year as a grid of dots, markable in place.
- Marks stored as a plain `roundel.csv`, exportable and importable.
- An emoji for what you are tracking.
- Days that have not arrived cannot be marked.
- Local only: no account, no network, no telemetry.
- Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel, signed and notarized.
